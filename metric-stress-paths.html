Metric / stress paths

How the pool responds

A price gap, missing quote, and one-sided flow create different problems. The response depends on the pool's setup.

01 / Event

Market reopens higher

The underlying stock jumps. The old quote may no longer reflect its price.

02 / Pool response

Update or reject

Quotes follow valid updates. Configured guards may reject affected swaps.

03 / LP position

Exposure remains

A new reference leaves token balances unchanged. Their dollar value can move.

A gap does not automatically stop trading. Valid quotes and the configured safeguards determine which swaps can execute.

Safeguards do not insure your deposit. StopLoss can reject certain swaps; it does not guarantee a sale price. Withdrawal conditions depend on pool extensions.
