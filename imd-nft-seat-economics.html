What if your worker also completes the work?

That is a separate bonus: 2% of launch supply is split equally across accepted-work wallets. With a 1B-token launch and five eligible wallets, each gets another 4 million tokens. The bonus depends on accepted work, not how many NFTs the wallet owns.

*Illustration: every launch issues 1B tokens; 8% is split equally by connected seats. Your one seat qualifies at every launch. Different projects issue different tokens: the monthly sum is a normalized allocation, not a single tradable balance or dollar income. Unlocks and liquidity matter.
